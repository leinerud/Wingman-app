Wingman 0.2 beta

  • Wingman now keeps itself up to date. New versions download quietly and install while you’re away from your Mac, so this is the last one you need to download yourself.
  • When an update is ready and you don’t want to wait, click Restart to update at the bottom of Wingman.
  • Prefer to decide yourself? Turn off Update automatically in Settings, or choose Check for Updates… any time.
