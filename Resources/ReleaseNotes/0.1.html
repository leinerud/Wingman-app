Wingman 0.1 beta

  • The first beta. See your GitHub Copilot sessions at a glance in the menu bar, the Dock and a desktop widget: what’s running, what needs you and what’s done.
  • Branches shows every branch your sessions used, what’s in each one in plain words, and which ones are done and safe to clean up.
  • Review shows what a session changed on screen, flags colours and sizes that aren’t in your design tokens, and previews your site as it changes.
